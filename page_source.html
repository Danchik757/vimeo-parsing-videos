Presumpscot Quilters Time Capsule Quilt 2025

11 months ago

Add to

City of Westbrook, Maine

On March 20th, 2025 the Presumpscot Quilters unveiled their incredible time capsule quilt at the Westbrook Community Center. It features landmarks and themes of Westbrook’s past and present. Former mayor Michael Sanphy also spoke and provided historical context for many of the images on the quilt. You will get a chance to check it out soon as it visits City Hall for display. It will also spend some time at Walker Memorial Library before eventually going to its forever home at the Westbrook Historical Society. Thank you to all the talented artisans who worked so hard to collaborate and create this masterpiece!
